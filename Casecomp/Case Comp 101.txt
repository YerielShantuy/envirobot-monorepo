Case Comp 101

Our Criteria: 

Evidence 
Every decision needs a number behind it 
Report each hole separately, don't average
Data beats assumptions, even if the terrain "looks" a certain way

Constraints
Read the question twice before talking
Assign someone to check the plan against every requirement
If a requirement's ambiguous, say how you interpreted it

Trade-offs
Every real plan gives something up
Name your weakness before a judge finds it
Ask: what did we say no to, and why was that worth it

Communication
Lead with your recommendation, not your process
Structure: recommendation → evidence → trade-off
Rehearse once, out loud, before judges arrive
One person should NOT team carry, everyone should pitch in (unless you have 6 people in your group)
