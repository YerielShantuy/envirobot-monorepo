# Case Comp — One Page

**Never average. Report each hole separately.** Stated twice in the brief = it's being tested.

## Clock (45 min)

| | Time | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Read | 5 | Every requirement written as a checkbox |
| Data | 10 | All readings on paper, per hole, sourced |
| Decide | 15 | Recommendation + the runner-up you rejected |
| Build | 10 | 4 beats in note form, speakers assigned |
| Rehearse | 5 | Said out loud, timed |

**Assign in the first 60s:** Timekeeper (calls boundaries aloud) · Requirements checker (at min 40, reads every requirement against the plan) · Devil's advocate (attacks your own answer — what they land becomes beat 4).

## Thresholds

**Soil (VWC %)** — Field capacity **20% sandy → 40% clay** = healthy. Permanent wilting point **~11% silt loam → 18% sandy clay loam** (fine soils 26–32%) = plants can't extract water; sustained below → death. Stress starts well above PWP.
→ *PWP depends on texture: 18% means something different in Desert than in Field.*
*(Oklahoma State Univ. Extension)*

**Water (NTU)** — **Australian Drinking Water Guidelines (NHMRC): <1 NTU at disinfection.** WHO: <1, max 5. 
→ **Above ~5 NTU turbidity shields pathogens from chlorine and clogs filters — people can't fix it by boiling or filtering.** That's why high NTU is a health emergency, not an aesthetic one.

## The 3-minute pitch — 4 beats

1. **Recommendation (20s)** — first words. "Our recommendation is X, then Y, Z, W."
2. **Evidence (60s)** — actual numbers, both holes cited, never averaged. One threshold citation.
3. **Comparison (60s)** — "We ranked Y second, not first, because…"
4. **Trade-off (40s)** — unprompted. "We're accepting that…"

Over 3 min → cut evidence, never the trade-off. Everyone speaks.

## Decision logic

Rank on the **worst reading** per zone, not the mean. Ask **what the intervention actually does** — then rank by *where one more week of waiting costs most, and whether that cost is recoverable*. Crop stress recovers next season; an outbreak doesn't. Say out loud how you interpreted anything ambiguous.

## Questions

- **"Why not average?"** → Averaging hides emergencies. 8% and 34% averages to a comfortable 21%.
- **"Best soil, why first?"** → The truck carries water. That zone's problem is water. Good soil is what makes it easy to miss.
- **"It's just cloudy, boil it."** → Above ~5 NTU turbidity blocks disinfection and clogs filters. Boiling isn't the fix.
- **"How confident?"** → Two-point soil calibration, turbidity zeroed on clear water, median of a ~3s window. Then give real uncertainty. Never claim precision you didn't measure.
- **"What would change your answer?"** → Have one ready. Knowing what flips your own recommendation reads as rigour.
- **"Weakest reading?"** → Name it before they find it.

## Say / Don't

✅ "Our recommendation is…" · "8% and 34%, not 21%" · "We're accepting that…" · "The question didn't define urgent, so we defined it as…" · "We ranked Y second because…"

❌ "So first we discussed…" · "The average was…" · "It looked pretty dry." · Any number you can't source · One person talking for 3 minutes
